About

I’m Abayomi, a graphic designer in Lagos. I build identities, posters and campaigns that are simple on the surface and deliberate underneath.

I run APEX Design, and I’m an Anatomy student at the University of Lagos, which is probably why I care so much about structure, proportion and how things fit together. Every project starts with a question: what should people feel in the first second?
